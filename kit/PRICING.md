# Pricing your game's things

You set your own prices, in crumbs, inside the ranges below. These ranges are bug.games' current guidance; they live in `pricing.json`, and bug.games can change them, so read the file rather than copying the numbers. Staff still approve every product before it sells.

**What a crumb is worth:** players buy crumbs in bundles: 500 for €4.99, 1,100 for €9.99, 2,400 for €19.99 and 6,500 for €49.99. So **100 crumbs ≈ €1** at the smallest bundle.

| What you sell | Range (crumbs) | ≈ € |
|---|---|---|
| Convenience: a revive at the point of failure, a skip for a real timer | 30–100 | 0.30–1 |
| Small look: sticker, emote, trail, frame | 100–250 | 1–2.50 |
| Look: skin, outfit, companion | 250–600 | 2.50–6 |
| Signature look: your best item (two at most) | 600–1,000 | 6–10 |
| Starter pack: 2–4 looks, once per player | 300–500 | 3–5 |
| Game pass: one season, about 30 steps | 600–1,000 | 6–10 |
| Chapter or world unlock (the first is free) | 300–800 | 3–8 |
| Creator subscription, a month | 150–500 | 1.50–5 |

**Rules:**
- Prices go in multiples of 10.
- If you price outside a range, write one line saying why. A person will look at it.
- Have at least one thing a player can buy with the smallest bundle (500 crumbs).
- Nothing you sell beats other players. Looks never change your game's identity core. Never stretch a timer to sell a skip.
- No fake discounts or "was" prices. No countdown unless something really ends. A limited item says when it returns.
- Prices never appear on your art: bug.games prints them.
