# Examples: four posters that work, and why

These four come from bug.games' own catalogue. They're here to show the grammar working, **not to copy**: never borrow their subjects, inks or compositions. Your poster should look like your game, the way these look like theirs.

See `examples/registers-and-small-sizes.png`: each poster wide, then at 90 px, then its icon 46 and emblem 30.

| Poster | Register | The one image | Light band (L / C) | Why it reads small |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon Body | Silhouette | a blue bust and its pink carbon copy | light .68 / .24 | Two big flat shapes in two inks. At 90 px it's still "a blue head and a pink double". The icon keeps just the head; the emblem keeps the stamp |
| Night Swim | Stage | a lighthouse beam finding a whale made of dots | dark .05 / .05 | One lit pocket in a black field. At 90 px the beam and the bright eye are what survive. The icon is the whale's lattice and gold eye, drawn at 46, not shrunk |
| Inkstrike | Edition (the loud one) | a black bomb-headed fighter on red rays | mid .48 / .55 loud | The one fluorescent exclamation of the wall. Rays and one dark mass still read at 90 px. Its red sits close to the platform's ember, so it's always rays and outlines, never a flat block near Play |
| Typos | Edition (the quiet sheet) | a creature typed in characters on white | light .95 / .04 quiet | The wall's rest. Almost empty, so the one figure carries it. The icon is the creature's face in a ring |

## What they have in common

- One image, decided before drawing.
- Two to four inks.
- The bottom strip is kept quiet for the caption.
- Lettering only where the poster can't shrink (Typos and Inkstrike are the two Edition posters; the others carry no title on the art).
- Small marks drawn for their size, each keeping the game's last survivors.
- Each looks like nothing else on the wall.
